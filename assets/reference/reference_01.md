# reference_01.mp4: "I went from THIS to THIS"

Before → after progress reel (surfing). 720×1280, 30 fps, 11.35 s, H.264 + AAC stereo; the audio is a music track.

## Structure
| Time        | Clip                                        | On-screen text            |
|-------------|---------------------------------------------|---------------------------|
| 0.0–2.6 s   | **Before:** wipeout, board flies up         | "I" → "Went" → "From" → "THIS" |
| 2.6–5.2 s   | **Before:** another fall in white water     | "I" … "From" … "This" (the words repeat) |
| 5.2–11.3 s  | **After:** paddles in, pops up, rides the wave | "To THIS" (shown ~5.2–7 s) |

There is one hard cut at ~5.17 s, where "before" switches to "after". The "after" clip is a single continuous shot, about 6 s long.

## Text style
- Serif font with title-case words, and "THIS" in all caps for emphasis
- White fill, thin black outline, no background box
- Words build up one at a time, about every 0.33 s, at staggered positions (the "I / Went / From" stack)
- The punchline sits in the upper third, centred, above the horizon: "THIS" at y ≈ 6–7 % of the height, "To THIS" at ≈ 17 %
